This Privacy Policy explains how Rayyan Ahmed (“I,” “me,” or “my”), the individual developer of MetaPilot, handles information when you use MetaPilot to connect an Instagram professional account and view its media and insights.

Information accessed

If you choose to connect an Instagram professional account, MetaPilot requests permission to access information needed for its read-only features. Depending on the permissions Meta grants, this may include your Instagram account ID, username and basic profile details; media identifiers, captions, timestamps and permalinks; and media or account insights such as views and other metrics made available by Meta.

MetaPilot does not use this connection to publish posts, manage comments, send messages, or create or manage advertisements.

How I use information

I use connected-account information to establish the connection you requested, display and synchronize Instagram media and insights, and provide analytics or explanations you request in MetaPilot. I do not sell connected Instagram information or use it for third-party advertising.

Authorization and access tokens

Authorization is handled through Meta. MetaPilot stores the authorization token needed to maintain your connection on its server and protects stored tokens using encryption. Do not send your password, App Secret, or access token to me by email or chat. You can revoke MetaPilot’s access through Meta’s settings; revocation may stop synchronization.

AI-assisted analysis

If an AI analysis feature is enabled for your account, the information needed to answer your request—for example, selected captions or performance metrics—may be sent to the AI service configured for MetaPilot. Do not use that feature to submit sensitive personal information. AI-generated output may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed before you rely on it. I do not authorize the AI provider to use your connected-account information to train its general-purpose models, subject to the provider’s applicable terms and configuration.

Sharing and service providers

I share information only as needed to operate MetaPilot: with Meta to authorize and retrieve the information you requested, and with infrastructure or AI providers configured to host the service or perform a requested analysis. Providers are expected to protect information and process it only for the relevant service. I may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the service, users, or others.

Retention and deletion

I keep connected-account information only while needed to provide the service, maintain your connection, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. To request deletion of your MetaPilot account data—including synchronized Instagram information and stored authorization credentials—email rayyan.ahmmed301@gmail.com from the address associated with your request and include your Instagram username. I will verify the request and delete or de-identify the requested information, except where retention is required by law. You may also revoke the app’s access in Meta settings.

Security

I use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption for stored authorization tokens. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please keep your Meta account credentials private and notify me if you believe your connection has been misused.

Children

MetaPilot is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. We will post the updated policy at this same address and revise the “Last updated” date above.
