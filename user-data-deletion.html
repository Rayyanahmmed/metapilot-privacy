How to request deletion of your MetaPilot data

MetaPilot is operated by Rayyan Ahmed as an individual FYP project. If you connected an Instagram professional account and want your MetaPilot data deleted:

  1. Email rayyan.ahmmed301@gmail.com from an address you can access.
  2. Use the subject MetaPilot data deletion request and include your Instagram username. Do not email your password, App Secret, or access token.
  3. I will verify the request and delete the stored MetaPilot account connection, encrypted authorization token, and synchronized Instagram data, unless retention is required by law.

You can also revoke the app’s access in your Meta or Instagram settings. Revocation prevents future access; contact me as above to request deletion of data already stored by MetaPilot.
